ML-303 ADAPTER ASSEMBLY JIG

Batch Production Assembly Jig Design

🔧 Purpose of This Jig

  • Hold multiple stripboard pieces for batch cutting
  • Align components for consistent assembly
  • Speed up wire routing and soldering
  • Ensure uniform quality across all adapters
  • Reduce assembly time from 5 minutes to 1 minute per adapter

Assembly Process Flow

1

Cut Stripboards

Use cutting jig to create 40 pieces of 5×5 hole sections

Time: 10 minutes total

2

Make Track Cuts

Drill all C3 positions using guide

Time: 5 minutes total

3

Pre-tin Pads

Apply flux and tin all connection points

Time: 15 minutes total

4

Cut & Strip Wires

Prepare 120 wires (3 per adapter)

Time: 20 minutes total

5

Solder Wires

Use wiring jig for consistent routing

Time: 30 minutes total

6

Test & QC

Verify all connections with test fixture

Time: 10 minutes total

Production Metrics

  • Total time for 40 adapters: ~90 minutes
  • Time per adapter: 2.25 minutes (vs 5 minutes without jig)
  • Success rate: 95%+ with jig (vs 80% freehand)
  • Cost savings: 55% reduction in labor time

Detailed Jig Dimensions

Critical Measurements

  • Stripboard slots: 13mm × 13mm × 2mm deep
  • Spacing between slots: 5mm
  • Wire guide channels: 1mm wide × 0.5mm deep
  • Alignment pins: 2mm diameter, 5mm height
  • Test probe spacing: 5.08mm (0.1") standard
