                     Pin 2 (Common)

STEP-BY-STEP BUILD:
==================
1. Cut stripboard to 4x10 holes
2. Make track cuts at column E, all rows
3. Solder two jumper wires:
   - B2 to F2 (blue wire)
   - B3 to G3 (black wire)
4. Add a bridge from F2 to G2 on bottom
5. Test continuity
6. Mount 4-pin switch on A2-B2, A3-B3

Materials:
- Stripboard piece: 10 x 4 holes
- 2x jumper wires (5mm stripped ends)
- Solder
- Track cutter or 3mm drill bit

Testing:
- 4-pin 1&2 should connect to 3-pin 1
- 4-pin 3&4 should connect to 3-pin 3
- 3-pin 2 connects to 3-pin 1
