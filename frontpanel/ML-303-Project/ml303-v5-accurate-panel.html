3-Pin Triangular Adapter Design

ML-303 Button Specifications

  • Original Buttons: Conrad-specific 12x12mm with 3-pin triangular footprint
  • Pin Layout: Triangular arrangement (not standard 4-pin square)
  • Status: Discontinued, no direct replacements available
  • Solution: Use standard 4-pin switches with custom adapter boards
