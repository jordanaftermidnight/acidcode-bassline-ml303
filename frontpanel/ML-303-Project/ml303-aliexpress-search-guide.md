## 📦 What to Look For in Listings

### ✅ Good Signs:
- Multiple product photos showing dimensions
- Clear pin layout diagrams
- "4 terminal" or "4 pin" in description
- Shows removable caps
- Bulk quantity options (50-100pcs)
- Seller responses in Q&A section

### ❌ Avoid:
- Single blurry photo
- No dimensions listed
- "SMD" or "SMT" type (need through-hole)
- Sellers with <95% positive feedback
- Shipping only from distant warehouses

